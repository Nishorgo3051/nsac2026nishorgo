We built a field map that keeps working when the network doesn't.

Download the region before you lose connectivity, and the map, the terrain and a real NISAR radar
flood layer all keep working offline: 128.4 km2 of new open water across south Chattogram, measured
from two NISAR L-band passes twelve days apart (30 June vs 12 July 2026).

It also tells you what it cannot see. Radar this way finds open water only, so flooded villages and
crops are missed and the figure is a lower bound. An empty layer is honest. Invented water is not.
