How we measured

    What this can't tell you

    • It's deliberately cautious. Only solid patches of land (roughly 90 m across or more) that connect to the old river bed count, so narrow bank strips are missed.
    • A sandy fallow field right beside the river can look like riverbed sand. Land that was river bed in either of the two previous photos isn't counted, which filters most of these out.
    • The long gaps (1989–2000, 2000–2010, 2010–2014) miss land that was taken and rebuilt in between, so they undercount.
    • 30 m pixels can't see single houses, and satellites can't see families. No people are counted here.
    • Upazila boundaries stay fixed while the rivers move. Land is counted in whichever upazila the official map puts it in.
    • The 2019 list may have been updated since. We couldn't find a newer version. None of these numbers have been checked against field records yet.
